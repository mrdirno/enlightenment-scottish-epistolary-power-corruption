## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write the full text of the Archivist's second letter, detailing another 'neat trick' of the Trust.
- Compose a diary entry from Elspeth's perspective, revealing what she suspects about her husband's late meetings.
- Draft the 'particularly rigorous' failing essay from the boy in the beanie, critiquing MacLeod's interpretation of civic virtue.
- Write the official Trust press release 'mourning' the closing of the Sutherland School of Traditional Arts, full of double meaning.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build an interactive map of Sutherland plotting the Trust's acquisitions, sales, and 'community projects' to visualize the corruption.
- Create a web-based 'Epistolary Engine' that generates threatening, period-appropriate anonymous letters in the style of the Archivist.
